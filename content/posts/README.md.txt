Blog posts live here as `<slug>.md`. A post exists on the site only when its file exists.

---
title: Headline of the post
description: One or two sentences for search results and the blog index.
tag: AI Search
---
Body in plain markdown: paragraphs, ## headings, - lists, **bold**, [links](https://example.com).

Author is always "eighty5labs". datePublished and dateModified come from the
git history of the file (scripts/update-lastmod.mjs), never typed by hand.
Every number in a post needs a source URL cited in the post.
