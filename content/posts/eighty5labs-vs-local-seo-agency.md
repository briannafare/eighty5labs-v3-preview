## What "AI search" actually changes

Less than the name suggests. [Google says](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) local results are "mainly based on relevance, distance, and prominence," and that "more reviews and positive ratings can help your business's local ranking." For its own AI answers, [Google says plainly](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) that "there are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary."

So there's no secret switch. What we do is keep the basics working every week: your profile stays current, reviews keep coming in and get answered, your FAQ pages answer what people really ask, and your site stays readable to the programs AI search tools send out to read websites. ChatGPT uses one called OAI-SearchBot, and [OpenAI says](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) sites that block it "will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers." Our job is to make your business easy to find and easy to cite. No one controls what an AI answer says, so we won't tell you we can get you into ChatGPT.

## When an agency is the better choice

If all you want is better website rankings, and you already answer your own calls and follow up on your own estimates, a good local SEO agency may be all you need.

If you have a large website with lots of pages, or you need link building, an agency that does that work is the right call, because we don't offer it.

And if you'd rather work with a company in your own city that you can sit down with, that's a fair reason to go local.

## Doing it yourself

You can do a lot of this on your own. Google's help pages for Business Profiles are free, and the basics of your profile take about an afternoon to set up. What it costs you is time, every week, to keep posting, asking for reviews and answering them. You know what your time is worth better than we do.
