## Do the math with your own numbers

Start with what one job, or one new patient, is worth to you on average. Then work out how many extra jobs a month it would take to cover the plan.

**(What one job is worth to you) x (extra jobs a month) = what those jobs bring in**

Put that next to the plan price. If a single job is worth more than the plan costs, one extra job a month covers it. If your jobs are smaller, you'll need a few, and you're the best judge of whether that's realistic for your business. We'd rather you work it out with your own figures than take a guess from us.

It probably isn't worth it if:

- you already answer every call yourself and your reviews come in steadily,
- you get very few leads to begin with, or
- most of what's in the plan is something you won't use.

## Which plan fits

If your problem is being found and being chosen, meaning people can't find you on Google or your reviews are thin, Visibility is the place to start.

If people are already finding you but calls go to voicemail and estimates go quiet, that's what Growth is for. Voice AI, missed-call text-back and follow-up are only in Growth.

## What isn't included

Voice AI and follow-up aren't in Visibility. Visibility and Growth each cover one location, so a second location is the $297 add-on, and businesses with several locations are usually a better fit for Dominate. If something isn't listed on this page, assume it isn't part of the plan and ask us.

## Other ways to get this done

**Doing it yourself.** Google's help pages for Business Profiles are free. What it costs you is your time, every week, to keep the profile and reviews going.

**An answering service.** If missed calls are the main problem, a live answering service puts a person on every call. We compare the two here: [Voice AI or an answering service](/blog/voice-ai-vs-live-receptionist).

**A local SEO agency.** If you only want better rankings and you handle calls and follow-up yourself, an agency may be enough. We compare the two here: [A local SEO agency or eighty5labs](/blog/eighty5labs-vs-local-seo-agency).

**Software you run yourself.** Review and messaging tools like Podium or Birdeye can cover part of this if you'd like to set it up and run it on your own. Their pricing pages have current prices.
