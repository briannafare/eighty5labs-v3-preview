## When a person is the better choice

Some calls need a human from the first word. If your calls are often emergencies, a distressed patient or a legal matter, someone who can hear what's going on and respond in the moment is worth paying for, and an answering service or a person on staff is the better fit.

If someone in your office already answers the phone all day, you probably don't need either of us during business hours.

And if you don't get many calls, a per-call plan can simply cost less. Smith.ai's Starter plan is $300 a month for 30 calls. Our Growth plan is $831 a month billed annually, with Voice AI, missed-call text-back and the rest of the plan included. Count the calls you actually got last month and put that number next to both prices before you decide anything.

## What we don't do

We don't replace your team. Voice AI is there for the calls nobody can get to, the overflow and the after-hours ones. We also can't promise what any one call turns into. What it does is answer, book the job when the caller wants it booked, and send you a text so you know what happened.

## Who should pick which

If you're out on jobs most of the day, calls come in after hours, and you'd rather have them booked than sitting in voicemail, Voice AI is a good fit, especially if you'd also like missed-call texts and follow-up on your estimates handled for you.

If your calls often need real judgment or a gentle voice in a hard moment, start with a live answering service. You'll pay more as the calls add up, but you're paying for a person on every one of them.

If you have the work and the budget to keep someone on the phone all day, hiring a person is still a great answer, and Voice AI can cover the nights and weekends they're off.
